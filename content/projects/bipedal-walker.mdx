## The goal

A small, buildable bipedal walker I can take from CAD all the way to a physical
robot: design in Onshape, simulate in MuJoCo, and eventually deploy control onto
real hardware.

## What's done: the CAD → sim pipeline

An automated four-stage conversion pipeline:

1. **Onshape** URDF export.
2. **URDF preprocessing** with compiler directives.
3. **MuJoCo compilation** to raw MJCF.
4. **Postprocessing** for RL readiness — actuators, sensors, floating base.

The result is reproducible: change the CAD, regenerate the sim model with one
command. Two variants are maintained (with and without the onboard Jetson).

## The hardware

- **784 g** (with Jetson) / 604 g (baseline).
- **6 actuated joints**, 2 passive, **24 sensors**.
- Jetson Orin Nano compute, ST3215 servos (position control, ±3 N·m).

## What's not done yet

**No balance controller.** Run the physics and the robot falls over. That's the
next milestone — and it's deliberately the honest framing here: this is a live
research project, not a finished result.
